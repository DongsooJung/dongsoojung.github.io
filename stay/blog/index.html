Your first 24 hours in Seoul

Before leaving the airport, confirm your route and terminal, connect your phone, and keep your lodging address in Korean. In the city, set up transit first; save shopping and complicated reservations for after you have checked in.

Arrival checklist

  1. Save your accommodation name, Korean address, and contact.
  2. Compare airport rail, limousine bus, and official taxi routes.
  3. Set up mobile data before relying on map or translation apps.
  4. Get the transit product that fits your actual itinerary.
  5. Keep passport and high-value items out of unattended luggage.

Cards, cash & tax refunds

International cards work widely in Seoul, but a small cash reserve remains useful at markets, tiny stalls, or when a payment terminal rejects an overseas card. Keep a second card separate from the first. For tax refunds, look for participating stores, retain the required documents, and confirm the current process before departure.

CarryTwo payment methods plus modest emergency cash.

AskConfirm tax-refund eligibility before paying rather than assuming every purchase qualifies.

KeepReceipts and purchased goods accessible if customs verification is required.

The small app stack that earns its space

Keep the stack lean: one Korea-capable map, one translation tool, your airline or airport app, and VISITKOREA for official tourism information. Download essential addresses and confirmations before moving, and do not place passport scans or full payment details into unknown travel apps.

OfflineScreenshot your lodging address, booking confirmation, and return flight.

Help lines worth saving before you need them

Save the Korea Travel Hotline and your accommodation contact before arrival. In an immediate emergency, use Korea’s official emergency services. For tourism questions and language help, the multilingual 1330 service is the most useful starting point; confirm current channels and language coverage on the official site.

Tourism help1330 Korea Travel Hotline (from overseas: +82-2-1330).

Police112 for urgent police assistance.

Fire & ambulance119 for fire or medical emergencies.

A low-friction Gangnam day from Daechi

Start with the neighborhood rather than crossing the city immediately. Walk Yangjaecheon or take a quiet café morning, continue to Seonjeongneung or Bongeunsa, and use COEX as a weather-proof afternoon option. Leave one unscheduled meal window; a flexible hour is often more valuable than another pinned attraction.

MorningDaechi streets or Yangjaecheon before commuter and academy peaks.

CultureChoose one of Seonjeongneung or Bongeunsa instead of rushing both.

Rain planCOEX and its connected indoor spaces reduce unnecessary transfers.
