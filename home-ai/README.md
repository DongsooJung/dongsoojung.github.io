# STARGATE HOME AI — English and Vietnamese beta landing pages

## Pages
- English: `/home-ai/en/`
- Vietnamese: `/home-ai/vi/`
- Shared responsive stylesheet: `/home-ai/assets/landing.css`

Both pages explain planned contract-term guidance, rental cost comparisons and a verification checklist. They show **proposed pilot prices** and a client-side calculator for deposits plus monthly charges. The calculator is informational, uses no external API, sends no input data and does not assess property safety. Early-access inquiries are `mailto:support@stargateedu.co.kr`.

## Live versus planned
**Implemented:** bilingual pages, language switch, responsive design, feature and pricing disclosures, calculator, contact email CTA, and disclaimers.

**Not implemented:** contract upload, OCR, AI contract review, authenticated back end, ownership/registry lookups, validated public rent transaction comparison, checkout, CRM signup, analytics or user account.

Do not imply that the proposed plans are available to buy or that AI can guarantee deposits.
