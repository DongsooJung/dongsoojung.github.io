Frequently asked questions

Can I upload a contract today?

No. Secure document processing, retention rules and an AI review backend are still being prepared. Do not email personal contract files as part of the beta inquiry.

Is STARGATE HOME AI a real-estate agent?

No. This service plans to provide general information, language assistance and data context. It does not arrange leases, provide regulated brokerage or individual legal advice.

Can you guarantee my deposit?

No. Any future report will describe available evidence and items requiring independent verification, not provide a guarantee.
